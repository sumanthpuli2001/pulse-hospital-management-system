How to Explain this Flow to the Interviewer

"Our system mirrors the real-world healthcare workflow across 6 distinct hospital roles:

1. Receptionist registers the patient and schedules the consultation.
2. Doctor conducts the clinical exam, logs ICD-10 diagnoses in the EMR, and prescribes medication.
3. Pharmacist inspects batch stock and fulfills medicines using FEFO (First-Expiring, First-Out).
4. Nurse manages the Bed Board and admits the patient to an available ICU bed with concurrency locks.
5. Accountant compiles consultation, bed, and drug charges into an invoice and records payment.
6. Administrator monitors live analytics, census KPIs, and security audit logs across the hospital."
